# Contacts: social and chat channels

Installs automatically with Contacts, Invoicing, Employees and Omnichannel.

- **Customers** filter (below Employees): contacts with a confirmed sale or a
  posted customer invoice (`customer_rank > 0`, Odoo's customer rule).
- **Social & chat tags** on the contact list (column) and kanban cards: the
  platforms where the contact has an active channel, coloured per platform.
- **Contact form**: brand chips (platform, account, link to the profile) under
  the main details, shown only when the contact has active channels; a
  **Social & chat** tab lists all of them, including archived ones.
- **Sources**: chats linked to the contact in Omnichannel (*Link existing
  contact* on a conversation) appear automatically and follow the link and the
  channel's active state; social profiles (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok…) or
  extra numbers are added by hand in the tab (Contact Creation rights).
- **Privacy**: everyone who can read the contact sees the platform tags and
  hand-added profiles; chat identifiers (numbers, chat IDs) are visible only to
  members of that chat's Omnichannel team.
- Filter **On social or chat**, and search by platform.
