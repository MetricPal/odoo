# Marketing: client portal

For advisors and agencies whose brands belong to clients.

- **Setup** (Brand › Settings › Client portal): add the client contacts and give them portal access from the
  contact (Action › Grant portal access). Optional: *Client signs off campaigns*, *Client reviews proofs*.
- **Portal** (`/my/marketing`, card "Marketing" on the portal home): a contact sees only the brands they are
  added to: what waits for them, results of the last 90 days, campaigns with status, channels out and results,
  proofs, and their requests. Budgets, spend and internal notes are not shown.
- **Campaign sign-off**: with *Client signs off campaigns*, the marketer clicks **Ask the client**; the client
  approves or asks for changes (comment required) in the portal. The campaign goes to the internal review only
  after the client approved; a refusal in the internal review asks the client again. The internal approval
  remains the only gate that releases anything.
- **Proofs**: proofs marked *Client reviews* are approved or sent back by the client in the portal (current and
  previous version side by side, with what changed); an approved proof fills the channel draft.
- **Requests**: the client files requests from the portal; they arrive in Work › Requests marked *Filed in the
  portal*, with a to-do for the brand's first team member.
- Every client decision is written on the campaign or proof as a message from the client contact.
